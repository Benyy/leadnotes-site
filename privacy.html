LeadNotes Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

LeadNotes is a browser extension that helps users save private notes, tags, statuses, and follow-up dates for LinkedIn profile URLs they manually visit.

Data we collect and store

LeadNotes may store the following information in your LeadNotes account:

  • Email address through Supabase Auth
  • Saved LinkedIn profile URLs
  • Manually entered display names
  • Manually entered headlines
  • Manually entered companies
  • Notes
  • Tags
  • Status values
  • Follow-up dates
  • Optional account, usage, company, tax, VAT, and billing information

Data we do not collect

LeadNotes does not collect or store:

  • LinkedIn passwords
  • LinkedIn session cookies
  • Scraped LinkedIn profile content
  • Automated LinkedIn activity
  • LinkedIn messages

No LinkedIn scraping or automation

LeadNotes does not scrape LinkedIn, does not use LinkedIn APIs, does not send LinkedIn messages, and does not automate LinkedIn activity. Users manually save and edit their own notes.

How we use data

We use your data to provide the LeadNotes service, sync your saved leads across devices, support account management, and understand product usage during beta. Optional business and billing details are collected for future invoicing and account management purposes.

Storage provider

LeadNotes uses Supabase for authentication and cloud database storage.

Data export and deletion

Users can export their saved lead data as JSON and delete all saved leads from the LeadNotes dashboard. Full account deletion is planned for production and will be handled through a secure server-side process.

Payments

LeadNotes does not currently process payments. Pro features are marked as coming soon.

AI

LeadNotes does not use AI in the current MVP.

Affiliation

LeadNotes is an independent tool and is not affiliated with LinkedIn.
